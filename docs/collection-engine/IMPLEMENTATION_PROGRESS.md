**Phase 0 audit complete with stated historical limits.** The aggregate reader is available, so benchmark coverage is now measured: 38 of 69 cases in a cohort reproducing the published totals have a complete current scoped Library reference, while 21 have a matching currently active Library version that had activated before the save. Under the current-schedule comparison three are partial, two have no scoped reference, and 26 lack a selected CPT. This does not measure newly supportable patient shares or policy-permitted collection; those require replay of original benefit evidence and actual NMCT policy approvals. A strict commercial-label cohort is 56 patients/67 cases; the totals-reproducing 57-patient cohort includes one Medicare-labeled payer, and the original membership manifest was not retained. Current rate/network records cannot reconstruct every October configuration revision.

Verification to date: the full backend suite passed **1,568 tests with 86 skipped** after the source-size fix; a subsequent focused trace/acquisition rerun passed 35 with one database skip. The two persistence tests passed against an isolated temporary PostgreSQL instance; its database was removed after the run. Frontend collection rendering passed 10 assertions; lint, type/build, formatting and size checks passed. The existing read-only tunnel was restored through an already running diagnostic ECS task. The `clinicforce_codex_ro` role reports `transaction_read_only=on` and no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE privileges on the inspected case-event and payer-connection tables. No paid payer API, live Bedrock request, production database write or deployment occurred. The feature flag remains off and Phase 6 is not ready for live NMCT collection without real approved policy rows.

The [deidentified original-snapshot counterfactual](../../../contextforcodex/collection-engine-october-replay-2026-11-02.md) used 69 final case snapshots and current active scoped BCBSTX rates in memory. It yielded nine numeric modeled shares, three positive; only one formerly null case gained a numeric result and that result was not positive. **Zero cases gained a policy-permitted positive collection decision.** This is a conservative current-configuration replay, not an October as-of reconstruction or evidence that old positive values were correct. The most frequent remaining gates are 31 cases without one complete insured service plan and 17 with identity, policy or service-date binding unresolved; 13 have a service-group cost-sharing interaction action. Missing source-backed deductible or copay interaction fields cannot safely be filled by arithmetic.
