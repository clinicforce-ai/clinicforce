Under the current-schedule comparison three are partial, two have no scoped reference, and 26 lack a selected CPT. This does not measure newly supportable patient shares or policy-permitted collection; those require replay of original benefit evidence and actual NMCT policy approvals. A strict commercial-label cohort is 56 patients/67 cases; the totals-reproducing 57-patient cohort includes one Medicare-labeled payer, and the original membership manifest was not retained.
