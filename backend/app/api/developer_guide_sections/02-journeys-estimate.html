What users do

Pick the journey closest to your task. Follow the action, the API request and the saved result.

Upload a record

Patient uploads a PDF → API extracts health data → original file is saved.

Share a summary

Patient creates a private link → clinician opens it with the PIN → summary appears.

Send an insurance card

Staff create an upload link → patient sends a card → staff review and confirm it.

Check a visit

Staff select a confirmed patient and visit → a workflow checks evidence → staff review the outcome.

Details and source links

Use these routes as waypoints. The exact models, optional fields, and errors appear in the generated reference below.

Clinician patients by appointment day. After clinic sign-in, Clinical patients reads the practice-owned dashboard. Each confirmed eCW-linked patient is checked through GET /api/staff/patients/{patient_id}/appointments?include_ecw=true, with at most three reads in progress. The list has Today’s patients, Upcoming patients and Past patients sections. Each section previews at most three date groups and offers See more; selecting it opens only that section with all of its dates paginated five groups at a time. Today naturally has one date. Returning to all patient dates restores the three previews. Upcoming dates remain in ascending order, including appointments beyond seven days. A patient appears once: a visit today takes priority regardless of time or completion status; otherwise the earliest future visit takes priority over history, and the most recent past visit is used when there is no current or future visit. Cancelled and entered-in-error eCW encounters are ignored. Upcoming and past patients are grouped under full weekday and date headings; the next seven days appear in date order, and past dates appear newest first. Search and card-status filters apply before grouping and to the selected section’s pages. Records without a readable appointment date remain visible under “Appointment date not available” in the Past patients section; that label does not assert a past visit. An unavailable eCW read is not proof of no visit. Refresh patients retries eCW reads and can move a returning patient to a newly booked date. Saved estimate dates, uploads and record-update times do not establish an appointment date. The per-patient endpoint enforces practice ownership and the confirmed eCW identity link, and no provider credentials or raw encounter payloads reach the list. See frontend/src/features/staff-workspace/ClinicalPatientDashboard.tsx, frontend/src/features/staff-workspace/ecwVisitDates.ts, and backend/app/api/routers/staff_flow/staff_appointments.py.

Patient uploads a health PDF

PatientPortal → POST /api/patient/upload → PDF extraction → HAPI FHIR transaction → S3 or encrypted Drive → patient_documents. The database row holds filename, location, and extracted FHIR JSON; the original PDF stays in object storage.

Patient shares a clinician summary

POST /api/patient/generate-link creates a SMART Health Link and encrypted shl_manifests row. The clinician scans a QR, enters the PIN, then POST /api/doctor/decrypt-and-summarize builds the summary and report. Shares can expire or be revoked.

Clinic receives a card

Staff creates POST /api/staff/upload-links; patient sends a card through POST /api/insurance-upload/{clinic_code}/{upload_code}. S3 holds the image, while insurance_upload_links records the receipt and staff_intakes records the unconfirmed patient data.

Staff checks a visit

Staff confirms the clinic patient, opens an eCW appointment, selects a workflow, and reviews the result. V1 uses documents; V2 submits payer resources; commercial previsit combines eligibility evidence with verified clinic rates. Each writes a scoped case and stage history.

Worked card example
  1. Create. A clinic worker signs in, chooses a patient-specific link, and receives an expiring private URL. The token is stored as a hash in insurance_upload_links.
  2. Submit. Fictional patient Alex uploads two card images with consent. The server saves originals to S3, creates a staff_intakes row, marks the link received, and returns a receipt. The intake still needs identity confirmation.
  3. Review. Staff opens GET /api/staff/intakes/{intake_id}, optionally runs card OCR, and uses PUT /api/staff/intakes/{intake_id}/patient to confirm the clinic patient. field_sources distinguishes patient entry, OCR, and staff decisions.
  4. Check. Opening V2 creates insurance_cases.workflow_json and an intake workflow reference. A coverage submission saves submitting before the payer call; pending means wait or refresh, not confirmed benefits.
eClinicalTouch / eClinicalMobile: QR to the patient chart

Purpose. A patient shares a snapshot from ClinicForce with a clinician using the receiving EHR's SMART Health Link importer. ClinicForce calls its no-passcode option eClinicalMobile compatibility test. eClinicalTouch and eClinicalMobile are separate receiving apps; record the exact app, version, EHR build and enabled practice feature when verifying compatibility.

What the vendor establishes. eClinicalWorks' April 9, 2026 production announcement describes scanning patient health QR codes with eClinicalMobile. Its official workflow demonstration shows the imported information as a PDF entry in PRISMA. The vendor's eClinicalTouch release history lists patient-shared SMART Health QR integration in version 4.19; the app requires EHR V12.0.2 or above. These sources establish the advertised workflow, not successful import of every ClinicForce resource or availability in every practice.

1. Create the snapshot

  1. Sign in to the patient portal, review the records and complete legal given name, family name and birth date. ClinicForce requires matchable demographics and at least one clinical/document entry beyond the Patient resource.
  2. For ClinicForce's clinician viewer, choose standard sharing and a four-digit PIN. For a receiving mobile-EHR test, choose Generate eClinicalMobile test QR and acknowledge the no-passcode sharing notice.
  3. POST /api/patient/generate-link authenticates the patient, reconciles Drive deletions and assembles a FHIR R4 collection bundle. Sources are saved structured records, extracted facts and document metadata, plus a live Epic snapshot if a live Epic session is connected. Source tags preserve their origin.
  4. The server creates a random 256-bit key, encrypts the bundle as compact JWE using alg=dir and enc=A256GCM, and saves encrypted data, expiry and optional PIN hash in shl_manifests. The QR contains the key; the manifest response does not.
ModeQR textAccess and destination
standardhttps://app.clinicforce.ai/#shlink:/<encoded-payload>PIN-protected; opens the ClinicForce viewer. Payload includes flag: P.
ecw_compatshlink:/<encoded-payload>Bare link, no PIN and no passcode flag. Intended for a compatible EHR importer. An ordinary phone camera may not handle this URI scheme.

The encoded JSON contains v, url, key, label and exp. The actual production manifest address is https://app.clinicforce.ai/manifest/<opaque-token>; staging uses https://staging.clinicforce.ai/manifest/<opaque-token>. PUBLIC_BASE_URL controls the host. Default expiry is three hours (SHL_SHARE_TTL_SECONDS=10800). The QR image is not a PDF or the entire medical record. Anyone with a no-passcode QR has the capability to fetch and decrypt that snapshot before expiry; keep it out of screenshots, tickets and logs.

2. Retrieve and decrypt after scanning

A conforming receiver decodes the base64url JSON after shlink:/ (or extracts it from the viewer fragment), checks expiry and sends an HTTPS POST to its url. Opening the manifest URL with a browser GET is not this exchange. The receiving vendor may perform retrieval/decryption on a mobile device or a service behind it; its public demonstration does not specify that internal placement.

// Synthetic receiver request body for ecw_compat; omit passcode
{"recipient":"Example receiving EHR","embeddedLengthMax":0}

// Standard shares also include the patient's PIN.
// The response contains one encrypted FHIR file:
{"files":[{"contentType":"application/fhir+json;fhirVersion=4.0.1",
            "location":"<temporary encrypted-payload URL>"}]}
// If embeddedLengthMax is omitted or large enough, "embedded" holds JWE instead.

The manifest validates availability and, for standard shares, the PIN. Its recipient field is required but is not a provider login or identity verification. A size-limited receiver receives a presigned S3 URL and must GET that encrypted object before the URL expires (one hour by default). S3 URLs use AWS Signature Version 4, required for the KMS-encrypted object; an older S3 signature can return HTTP 400 even when manifest retrieval succeeded. The receiver then uses the QR key to authenticate/decrypt the JWE and parse FHIR. The original key never belongs in the manifest request body, query string or logs. Manifest expiry prevents new resolution; an already-issued object URL can remain usable until its own expiry, and previously retrieved data cannot be recalled.

3. What “populate the chart” means

The receiving EHR owns patient matching, authorization to modify its chart, duplicate handling, reconciliation and display. ClinicForce supplies demographics and FHIR resources; it does not issue a chart-write call to the practice in this QR route. A ClinicForce Patient ID is a portal identifier, not an eCW chart ID. Verify the selected/matched patient in the receiving workflow; do not invent an automatic match rule from names alone.

Identity limitation in current compatibility code. apply_identity_assurance() adds IAL2/high-confidence labels and the IDI-Patient-L2 profile marker during compatibility assembly, without checking an ID.me verification result in this path. Those labels are implementation metadata, not proof of an identity-proofing event or validated profile conformance. They must not be used as evidence that the vendor's verified-identity prerequisite has been satisfied.

The vendor demo establishes a document-import result. It does not establish automatic reconciliation into discrete EHR fields, exact supported FHIR profiles, trusted issuer/domain onboarding requirements, or ClinicForce-specific acceptance. The standard ClinicForce viewer's POST /api/doctor/decrypt-and-summarize and generated report are a separate workflow; the mobile importer does not need to call that endpoint.

4. Verify each boundary and diagnose a failure

  1. Generation: verify authenticated generation returns the correct bare/wrapped format, host, expiry and PIN flag. Decode the PNG with an independent QR reader and compare its text to the returned SHL.
  2. Transport: resolve the manifest through CloudFront, test embedded and download-URL forms, decrypt with the QR key and verify expected FHIR content in memory. Check invalid PIN, unavailable share and malformed requests without printing payloads.
  3. Mobile import: record the app/version, EHR build and practice feature; scan a synthetic case with matching demographics. Confirm the intended chart, PRISMA document, expected facts and persistence after refresh. If discrete fields are required, verify them individually.
  4. Evidence: record only pass/fail, time, environment and support references. Research establishes vendor-documented behavior; a local conforming receiver establishes transport; only an observed receiving-app result establishes that installation's chart population.

Generation failure: check authentication, demographics and source content. No edge request: check the scan feature, bare SHL support, device connectivity and log delivery delay. Edge error with no backend event: inspect CloudFront routing/origin result. Backend 422: request schema (including required recipient); 401: PIN; 404: unavailable/expired/revoked share; 503: encrypted-object storage. HTTP 200 with no imported document: investigate receiver decryption, FHIR acceptance, patient matching and importer configuration. A fetch success cannot certify chart population.

Use QR and CloudFront diagnostics. Implementation: API routes, snapshot persistence, SHL/JWE encoding, exchange bundle, and patient QR controls.

Follow one visit estimate

A fictional patient, Alex Example, has an upcoming visit. Staff need visit details, insurance benefits and a clinic rate before they can show an estimate.

Example only$200 allowed→$50 deductible + $30 coinsurance=$80 estimated patient share

Here, 20% coinsurance applies to the remaining $150. Real estimates also depend on network, benefit limits, effective dates and other rules.

Details and source links

This is a fictional teaching case, not a live patient or proof that a provider is available. Dr. Lee signs into the clinic workspace and enters Alex Example, born January 15, 1980. Suppose eCW returns one matching patient and a visit two days from today, and the clinic has one active, verified in-network policy and a reviewed rate for CPT 99213, one unit. For this example there is no usable saved eligibility, so a Stedi check is needed. Name and birth date alone cannot establish insurance benefits or a price.

FHIR is the JSON-based health-record format used by eCW and HAPI. An Encounter represents the visit used by this reader; Coverage describes a policy. Eligibility is commonly called a 270 inquiry / 271 response. Stedi’s JSON API carries the inquiry to the payer and returns benefits. A CPT/HCPCS code identifies a service; an NPI identifies a provider; the allowed amount is the clinic’s negotiated price. Learn FHIR R4 and Stedi eligibility.

eCW patient suggestions and correcting saved identity

A missing exact match does not prove that the entered details are wrong or that the patient is absent. The staff lookup returns 200 {status: "selection_required", candidates: [...]} with up to eight suggestions and Cache-Control: no-store. An empty list invites a refined search. Inexact or ambiguous eCW matches create no intake and read no encounters or benefits before selection. A conflicting saved clinic identity can also require confirmation after an exact eCW lookup; that lookup may already have read the verified eCW patient’s visits, but does not change the saved clinic identity. Provider authorization, transport and timeout failures remain errors rather than empty search results.

The initial search uses the documented given/family/birthdate, family/birthdate and name/birthdate combinations. An ecw_account_number with DOB searches the secondary identifier and birth date, then verifies the returned secondary ACCT. First and last names can be blank, including for a patient ClinicForce has never linked. The returned name and DOB are shown for explicit staff selection before encounters are read or an intake is saved. ACCT without DOB returns 422; eCW does not support secondary-identifier-only search. The unsupported given+birthdate-only fallback is removed. For searches without an ACCT, bounded name-only fallback queries read only the first page (up to 100 rows per name); they never enumerate the practice roster. Suggestions are not exhaustive. Use a more precise name or the account number when the intended patient is not shown.

First-name or surname suggestions permit one insertion, deletion, substitution or adjacent swap, with at least four normalized characters, exact DOB and the other name matching (including the existing compound-surname tail rule). Conflicting supplied middle initials exclude those suggestions. DOB differences require both names to match locally, or a verified exact account. An entered account excludes other accounts. Similarity only orders the dropdown; it never confirms identity. Ordered multiword given names are compared in full.

The dropdown shows the eCW name, DOB and account, with differences from any supplied fields identified below it. Nothing is preselected, including ACCT-and-DOB results. Staff choose a patient and acknowledge identity before continuing. Changing selection clears acknowledgement; editing search fields, leaving the search or losing the session clears suggestions. The confirmation POST sends selected_patient_id, returned canonical demographics, displayed account when available and identity_confirmed: true. The server re-reads that ID and checks the demographics and account before reading encounters or saving; changed selections return 409. A similarity score is not a probability that two people are the same.

New intakes retain eCW's canonical name. Explicit confirmation can update the linked ClinicForce patient's demographics only for the same eCW ID and practice; it records a staff event, clears prior insurance identity approvals if demographics changed and updates that intake's demographic source fields. A changed intake identity clears its prior portal association so corrected eCW demographics cannot inherit another portal identity. It never relinks an existing patient to a different eCW ID or rewrites saved insurance snapshots. A conflicting saved identity requires confirmation even when the new search itself is an exact match. Initial and saved-link checks apply the same surname rule, so a successfully matched compound surname does not fail solely because the saved display name used its final word.

Commercial appointment listing and previsit reads return 409 with code: "ecw_patient_selection_required" for an unresolved eCW identity. The estimator offers Find matching eCW patients, opening the staff search with the saved details. Staff confirm a result before returning to its visits. Selecting another person opens that person's intake; the original patient and saved estimate stay attached to their original records. No payer request is made by suggestion search or confirmation.

Worked calculation: a synthetic $80 patient estimate

Assume one confirmed service, one active primary plan, individual benefits, verified in-network participation, no secondary coverage, no unresolved authorization/referral/identity issue, and a current clinic contract. The clinic rate is $200. The applicable remaining deductible is $50, coinsurance is 20%, the rule is coinsurance with no added copay, and remaining out-of-pocket allowance is $1,000. All are fictional inputs; missing terms must not be defaulted to these numbers.

Allowed amount:                      $200.00  (clinic contract)
Deductible applied: min($200, $50) =   $50.00  (payer benefit balance)
Amount after deductible:              $150.00
20% coinsurance on $150:                $30.00
Patient estimate: $50 + $30 =           $80.00
Plan estimate: $200 - $80 =            $120.00
Patient amount is capped by the allowed amount and remaining out-of-pocket limit.
Stored/calculated in integer cents: 20000, 5000, 3000, 8000, 12000.

Failure and recovery branches to understand

An eCW Patient timeout stops identity resolution: the staff API normally returns 502 with a request ID when the shared 24-second lookup deadline is reached; the staff route also has a 26-second outer bound. These bounds describe patient/appointment listing, not a guaranteed 24-second limit for the entire multi-stage previsit request. No appointment or Stedi result can be inferred from that failure. A completed query with zero appointments is different from a query that failed.

Missing payer/coverage identity can initiate background Stedi insurance discovery when required demographics/provider identity exist. Candidates are filtered against the patient. A unique active medical policy matching the patient and a current or recorded prior address can recover missing or obsolete eCW policy details. Multiple active policies require reconciliation; discovery order does not establish primacy. The UI polls the saved case; it does not invent insurance. Explicit request-shape rejection may cause a bounded retry without optional identity hints; a self-subscriber AAA 73 name rejection may retry member ID plus DOB. A transport/parse failure after submission is indeterminate and must be reconciled against payer activity before another submission. See _post_stedi_eligibility, _begin_coverage_discovery and _start_previsit_recovery.

A successful benefit response with no matching contract rate still cannot establish a negotiated insured amount. Conflicting identity, multiple coverages, unknown network, service/date mismatch, or stale evidence can require review. The result may be HTTP 200 with issues, no price, or deferred collection guidance. A displayed $0 requires a supported calculation; missing eligibility or secondary coverage alone cannot establish zero liability.

The commercial summary keeps unresolved network participation and referral or authorization rules visible as amber review items. A network name or clinic record alone does not confirm this member's in-network status; the green network state requires a supported payer participation message or a current matched Clinical Library contract. A required referral without a recorded approval and a rule whose approval state is unknown both remain review items. A completed electronic check that cannot verify coverage says Coverage unverified · review required; an automatic cash fallback says Coverage unverified · cash pricing, without treating the patient as uninsured. Saved estimate history opens assumptions and evidence disclosures so the archived basis can be read alongside the frozen result.
